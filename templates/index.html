Psychological Spending Takeaways

Positive Association (ρ = +0.318): Students susceptible to spending because "friends are doing it" display a statistically significant likelihood of also spending on food and entertainment when stressed about academics.
Discretionary Core Drivers: Eating Out / Cafes (66.4%) and Public Transit (61.8%) represent the two dominant weekly cash outflows, followed by Academic Supplies (34.4%).
Digital Subscriptions: Nearly 1 in 5 students maintains recurring entertainment subscriptions (Spotify, Netflix, Prime), often representing unnoticed passive outlays.
